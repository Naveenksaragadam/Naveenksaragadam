## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/WUlplcMpOCEmTGBtBW/giphy.gif" width="30" alt="Waving Hand"> &nbsp;About Me

I'm a **Senior GenAI Engineer** at **JPMorganChase** with **10+ years of experience** designing, developing, evaluating, and maintaining production-grade Generative AI applications, enterprise Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, and LLM-powered services across financial services, insurance, telecommunications, and healthcare.

- 🔭 &nbsp;Currently building an enterprise RAG platform at JPMorganChase — indexing **~250,000 documents** and processing **5,000 queries per day**, with **92% answer correctness, 95% citation accuracy, and 90% Recall@5** on a curated evaluation set of 1,000 questions
- 🚀 &nbsp;**~30% lower average response latency** through retrieval tuning and caching, and **~20% lower LLM cost per query** through prompt optimization and more efficient context construction
- 🌱 &nbsp;Specializing in **RAG pipelines, LLM evaluation (RAGAS), agentic workflows with human-in-the-loop approval, and MLOps** — plus enterprise data engineering on Azure Databricks, Spark, and Snowflake
- 🏆 &nbsp;Certified: **Azure Databricks Data Engineer Associate · Microsoft Fabric Data Engineer Associate · AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate · Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate**
- 🎓 &nbsp;**MS in Computer Science**, University of Arizona
